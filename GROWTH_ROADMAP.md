# Growth Roadmap

## First 30 days: measurement and trust

- Verify GA4 event quality and create funnels for start → progress → completion → result → share.
- Connect Search Console, submit the 103-URL sitemap, and monitor redirect/canonical processing.
- Review real-user Core Web Vitals, crawl errors, email delivery failures, and Stripe checkout completion.
- Unpause and verify the live Supabase project, then apply the locked-down RLS migration and deploy the rate-limited email function.
- Establish a monthly editorial review for testing claims, citations, corrections, and celebrity-estimate evidence.

## Days 31–60: retention and utility

- Add an opt-in daily reasoning challenge with a finite, reviewed question bank and local streak history.
- Expand the score interpreter with test-specific scale presets only where documentation supports them.
- Add an accessible “continue learning” collection based on user-selected interests, not inferred personal traits.
- Test result-card formats and challenge copy while keeping exact score sharing user-initiated.
- Build a privacy-respecting internal search and track `internal_search` only after it exists.

## Days 61–90: authority and distribution

- Publish fewer, stronger source-led guides addressing validated testing, measurement error, practice effects, and when professional assessment is appropriate.
- Seek expert review for high-stakes testing content and display reviewer credentials only after verification.
- Earn links through genuinely useful tools, transparent datasets, and embeddable charts rather than programmatic page volume.
- Evaluate AdSense only after approval, stable traffic, and policy review; activate the conservative map without quiz ads.
- Run structured experiments on homepage messaging, assessment completion, interpreter usage, and share rate with explicit guardrails for trust and speed.

## Ongoing guardrails

- No clinical, diagnostic, employment, or school-placement positioning.
- No unsupported celebrity, demographic, nationality, or career intelligence claims.
- No index expansion without unique intent, original value, and an identified maintenance owner.
- No ad or conversion experiment that obscures free results or interrupts the assessment.
- No new third-party script without privacy, performance, and failure-mode review.
